the idea

You don't need advice.
You need to feel heard.

A lot of people struggle in silence — not because they don't want help, but because they don't feel safe asking for it. Most apps respond to that with checklists, journaling prompts, and streaks. Being does the opposite.

"A tree simply is. It goes through every season, every storm, without trying to be anything other than itself."

— the design brief behind Being

Being is a quiet presence. It asks nothing of you when you open it. It waits. When you're ready, it listens — and reflects back what it hears without judgment, advice, or hollow affirmations.

what it does

Built around presence,
not productivity.

Voice-first conversation
Speak naturally. Being listens using OpenAI Whisper, transcribes what you said, and responds — both in text and voice — with a warm, human tone.
AI that listens, not lectures
Powered by Claude (Haiku). Responses are 2–4 sentences max. Always lowercase. No advice unless you ask. No "I hear you." Just honest, gentle presence.
The Being Tree
A tree made of triangles lives at the heart of the app. Slowly animated. Quietly present. A symbol of something that simply exists, season after season.
Live voice waveform
When you speak, animated bars pulse with your voice level — a subtle visual cue that Being is genuinely listening to every word.
Private by design
Everything is stored locally on your device via AsyncStorage. No accounts. No cloud sync. Your conversations belong to you, and only you.
Gentle check-ins
After a period of silence, Being quietly checks in — "hey, you still around?" — without pressure, without urgency. Just making sure you know it's there.
